Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

HomeBar is a macOS menu bar app for monitoring and controlling Home Assistant devices. Preferences and authentication tokens are stored locally on your Mac.

Data Collection

HomeBar does not collect, track, or transmit personal data to the developer. No analytics or advertising services are used.

Local Storage

Authentication tokens are securely stored in the macOS Keychain.

Preferences and entity configurations are stored locally on your Mac.

Home Assistant data is exchanged directly between HomeBar and your configured Home Assistant server.

No user data is sent to servers operated by the developer.

Data Sharing

HomeBar does not share your data with the developer, advertisers, or analytics providers.

Data Deletion

You can remove your locally stored preferences and authentication tokens from your Mac. HomeBar does not maintain any user data on external servers.

Contact

For any question, please open an issue on the HomeBar GitHub repository or send an e-mail to contact [at] jebrg.com.
